Description:Cristiano Ronaldo is a Portuguese professional footballer widely regarded as one of the greatest players of all time. Born on February 5, 1985, he has played for top clubs including Sporting CP, Manchester United, Real Madrid, Juventus, and Al-Nassr. Known for his incredible goal-scoring ability, speed, athleticism, and dedication, Ronaldo has won multiple Ballon d'Or awards and numerous league and Champions League titles. He is also the all-time top scorer for both Portugal and the UEFA Champions League.
