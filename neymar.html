Description: Neymar Jr. is a Brazilian professional footballer known for his flair, dribbling skills, creativity, and goal-scoring ability. Born on February 5, 1992, he began his career at Santos FC before moving to Europe with FC Barcelona, where he formed a famous attacking trio with Messi and Suarez. Later, he joined Paris Saint-Germain (PSG) in a record-breaking transfer. Neymar has been a key player for Brazil, winning the Olympic gold medal in 2016 and playing in multiple World Cups. He is regarded as one of the most talented and entertaining players of his generation.

Nationality: Brazil

Achievement :

  • Champions League
  • Olympic Gold medal
  • Fifa Confederations Cup Golden Ball
