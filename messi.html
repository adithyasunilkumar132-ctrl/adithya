Description:Lionel Messi is an Argentine professional footballer, widely regarded as one of the greatest players of all time. Born on June 24, 1987, in Rosario, Argentina, he plays as a forward for Inter Miami and the Argentina national team. Messi spent most of his career at FC Barcelona, where he won numerous titles including 4 Champions Leagues and 10 La Liga title.

Nationality: Argentina

Achievement:

  • 2022 world cup winner
  • Champions League winner
  • Copa America winner
  • Ballen d'or winner
